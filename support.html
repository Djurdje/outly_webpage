Common questions

The Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy for the app are written in Slovenian; where this page summarises them, the Slovenian text prevails.

Tickets

How do I buy a ticket?

Open an event and tap the price on the poster. Choose how many tickets you want, then tap Pay (or Get ticket if the event is free). A secure Stripe page opens for the payment and brings you back to Outly. Your tickets appear under Profile → Tickets as soon as the payment goes through, each with its own QR code. Events marked 18+ need a date of birth in your profile. Outly never sees or stores your card details.

Where available, you can also buy without an account in the web app. The confirmation and the tickets arrive by email at the address you enter, and a private link opens your order; the link works until 30 days after the event (Terms of Use, section 4).

How do I use my ticket at the door?

Open Profile → Tickets, tap the ticket and show the QR code to the staff at the entrance. They scan it with Outly. A ticket can be scanned only once. In the iPhone app, Tickets keeps the last list it downloaded, so you can still open your QR code without a signal. The club may ask for an ID.

Can I get a refund?

  • A ticket is for a specific date, so there is no 14-day right of withdrawal and a ticket cannot be returned because you changed your mind (Terms, section 7).
  • If the event is cancelled, you are entitled to a refund of the ticket price. The organiser makes the refund; Outly starts it on your behalf and tells you how it goes. It goes back to the payment method you used.
  • If the event is postponed, the ticket normally stays valid for the new date. If the new date does not suit you, you can ask for a refund within 14 days of the notice (Terms, section 8).
  • For anything else, contact the club or write to us with your ticket number.

Can I send a ticket to a friend?

Open the ticket in Profile → Tickets, tap Send to a friend, choose a friend (or Send by email) and confirm. The ticket moves to their account with a new QR code and your old code stops working. Tickets can be sent until the event starts; guest list tickets cannot be sent. Your friend must meet the event’s age limit. A ticket bought without an account cannot be sent until you add the order to your account (Terms, section 4). If the event is cancelled, the refund goes to the original buyer.

Is there an age limit?

You must be at least 15 to create an account. Some events have a higher limit, shown on the event page. Your date of birth is not proof of age: the organiser checks your ID at the entrance (Terms, section 3).

Your account

I forgot my password

On the login screen choose Forgot password and enter your email. In the iPhone app and the web app we send you a code, which you enter together with a new password. On this website we send you an email with a link that lets you set a new password. In the apps, if you are signed in, go to Profile → My Account → Password and security; changing the password signs you out on your other devices.

How do I report or block someone?

In the iPhone app: to report an event or a club, tap the … menu at the top right of its page, choose Report, pick a reason and tap Send. To report or block a person, open the … menu next to their name (in My Friends or when you search for friends) and choose Report or Block. You can undo a block under Profile → My Account → Blocked users.

In the web app: on the page of a club or an event, tap Report, pick a reason and send it. To report or block a person, open the menu next to their name in your friends list. You can undo a block under Profile → My Account → Preferences → Blocked users.

People you block cannot find you or send you requests, and they are not notified.

If you think content on Outly infringes your rights, write to us at luka@outly.si and we will review it and remove it if needed (Terms, section 11).

For clubs

To list your club, open Profile → My Account → Request for creator, fill in the short form and follow the status on the same screen. We review requests by hand. You can also apply on the creator page.

Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time:

  • In the iPhone app: Profile → My Account → Delete account. Enter your password to confirm, tap Delete Account and confirm.
  • In the web app (outly.si/app): Profile → My Account → Delete account, with the same steps.
  • On this website: sign in, open your profile, choose Delete account, enter your password and confirm.
  • By email: write to luka@outly.si from the address you signed up with, or tell us which address to look for. We answer within one month at the latest.

Your personal data is removed. Records of past purchases are kept in anonymised form, as accounting rules require. Valid tickets on a deleted account cannot be recovered, so use them or send them to a friend first. A club owner whose club has sold tickets must first transfer ownership of the club; until then the account cannot be deleted. Details: Privacy Policy, section 6.
