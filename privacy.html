Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · version 2.4.2 · applies to the Outly app, the web app at outly.si/app and outly.si

This policy explains what personal data Outly collects — on this website (waitlist, your account, creator applications), in the Outly app and in the web app at outly.si/app (account, friends, tickets) — why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf and what rights you have. The Slovenian version is at outly.si/privacy-app; if the two differ, the Slovenian version prevails.

Version 2.4.2 adds the web app at outly.si/app (browser storage, location, camera for ticket scanning) and corrects the description of sign-in security. It describes processing the web app already does; it adds no new purposes.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is NEXT DIMENSIONS, družba za marketing, d.o.o., Trebče 81, 3256 Bistrica ob Sotli, Slovenia, registration number 7238274000 (“Outly”, “we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or about your data, contact luka@outly.si.

Event organisers are separate controllers. When you buy a ticket in the app, the organiser of that event receives the data it needs to sell the ticket and let you in. The organiser is responsible for that data under its own privacy policy.

2. What we collect

Waitlist (this website)

  • your email address, the time you signed up and the time you confirmed it;
  • a random confirmation token used only to verify that the inbox is yours;
  • if you came through a friend’s invite link: the invite code, so we can count the invite (one point for your friend once you confirm). We do not tell your friend your address.

Account (website, app and web app) — one account works in all of them.

  • your email address and username;
  • your password, stored only as a cryptographic hash (bcrypt) by our authentication provider Supabase — we never see or store the password itself;
  • email confirmation and password-reset codes, valid for a limited time;
  • when you sign up on this website or in the web app: which version of the Terms of Use you accepted, and when;
  • when you complete your profile in the app: phone number (optional), date of birth (used for event age limits), country, the music genres you pick, a profile picture if you upload one;
  • events you save with the heart (only you see them) and clubs you follow (the club sees only the number of followers, not who they are); in-app notifications and whether you have read them.

Friends in the app

  • friend requests and friendships (who, to whom, when, status). Other users can find you by username and see only your username and profile picture — never your email, phone or date of birth;
  • plans visible to friends: your friends can see which upcoming events you have a ticket for (“going”) and where you tapped “I’m in” (“interested”) — only your friends, never other users, and only while the Friends see my plans switch in settings is on. You can turn it off, remove a friend or remove an “I’m in” at any time;
  • ticket transfers: who sent a ticket to whom and when. The rules are in the Terms of Use.

Using the app and the web app

  • your IP address, used to limit requests from one address (for example purchases, ticket transfers, searches and friend requests) and so prevent abuse; we keep it in our database only as an irreversible hash (HMAC). Sign-in and sign-up attempts are limited by our authentication provider Supabase;
  • technical server error logs and error and crash reports (see Sentry in section 4).

Buying a ticket

  • buyer email, event, number of tickets, amount, date and payment status;
  • the payment identifier at our payment provider;
  • when and where the ticket was scanned at the door.

We never receive, process or store your card details. You enter your card directly into the payment provider’s form.

Creator applications and business accounts

  • the business and contact details you enter in the creator application (business name, type, address, city, licence ID, contact name and role, email, phone, message);
  • for approved clubs: club name, address, contact email and phone, website, Instagram, map coordinates, logo, photos and videos; the email address and role of staff the owner invites (manager, door); the account identifier and onboarding status at the payment provider.

Ticket scanning in the web app (club staff)

  • Camera. Your browser asks for the camera only when you open the scanning screen. The camera image is processed by the browser on your device; it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Only the content of the scanned QR code is sent to our server to check that the ticket is valid. The camera turns off when you leave the screen or close the tab. You can revoke access in your browser settings at any time.
  • Scanning without a connection. So the door keeps working when the internet drops, the staff member’s browser keeps a list of tickets for events currently taking place: ticket code, status, entry time, table and package and the holder’s username (for guest lists also the host’s username) — no email addresses. The list is deleted when the staff member logs out, otherwise the first time the web app is opened three days after it was last downloaded. Scans not yet sent to the server stay in the browser until they are sent; sent scans stay for three days so the device can recognise a second entry, and are deleted the first time the web app is opened after that.
  • Who scanned. With each scan we store the time, the staff member who scanned and a device marker: for online scans the browser or app type, for offline scans a random marker the browser creates when the scanner is first opened and that is not linked to anything else.

Web app (outly.si/app). The web app is the browser version of the Outly app. It uses the same account, server and data as the app, so everything this policy says about the app also applies to it. What it keeps in your browser is described in section 10.

Location. The app asks for your device location the first time you open the map. In the web app your browser asks only when you tap “Use my location”; if you have already allowed it, the web app reads your location by itself when it opens. Your location is never sent to our servers or stored — it is used only on your device to centre the map and sort clubs by distance; in the web app it stays in the open page and is not saved in the browser. You can refuse or revoke access in your phone or browser settings at any time.

The web app’s map uses OpenStreetMap data served from our own domain outly.si; map requests do not go to any map provider. Your browser downloads from our domain the parts of the map for the area you are looking at. Club owners can set the club’s location in the web app by clicking on the map; these coordinates are part of the club’s details.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Waitlist email — to tell you when Outly launches in your city and to send the confirmation email. Basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by entering your address and clicking the confirmation link. You can withdraw it at any time (section 8).
  • Invite counting — so your friend gets a point when you confirm. Basis: our legitimate interest in running the invite programme (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Email, username, password, confirmation codes — running your account and signing you in. Basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Date of birth — event age limits. Basis: contract and the organiser’s legal obligation.
  • Phone number — purchase notifications and account recovery. Basis: contract; consent where entering it is optional.
  • Country, genres, saved events, followed clubs — recommending clubs and events, notifying you about new events. Basis: contract.
  • Friends and ticket transfers — connecting with friends, sending tickets. Basis: contract.
  • Plans visible to friends (tickets and “I’m in”) — the friends’ plans section. Basis: consent, given with the switch in settings; you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Email delivery data (delivered, opened, clicked) — checking that our emails arrive. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)); never used for advertising or profiles.
  • Profile picture — shown in your profile. Basis: consent.
  • IP address and logs — preventing abuse and password guessing. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security of the service.
  • Error and crash reports — fixing what breaks in the app, the web app and on our server. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchase data — completing the purchase and issuing the ticket (contract); keeping it afterwards for accounting and tax (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Ticket scanning — preventing double entry. Basis: contract.
  • Ticket list on staff devices, device marker with each scan — scanning at the door without a connection, resolving double entries. Basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in reliable entry.
  • Data in your browser (session, language, preferences; section 10) — making the service you use work. Basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); storing it in the browser is strictly necessary for the service you request, so it needs no consent (Article 225 of the Slovenian Electronic Communications Act, ZEKom-2).
  • Creator applications — assessing whether to work together. Basis: steps prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Recommendations are based on the genres you pick and the events you mark. We do not make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR), and we never sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under data processing agreements and only on our instructions:

  • Supabase — the waitlist database and account authentication in the app, the web app and on this website (email, password hash; your IP address when you sign in, to limit attempts). Data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) — emails from this website (waitlist confirmations, account emails). Brevo records delivery events and whether an email was opened or a link clicked; we use this only to check that our emails arrive.
  • Render — hosting of the app’s server and database (account, profile, saved events, tickets, purchases, creator applications). Servers in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Resend — emails sent from the app and about creator applications.
  • Cloudinary — storing and serving uploaded images and videos (profile pictures, club logos, posters and videos). The web app uploads and shows them directly from Cloudinary’s servers, which receive the technical data any web server receives, such as your IP address.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Amount, email and card details, entered directly with Stripe, never through us.
  • Event organiser — your email, your tickets and whether you entered.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of this website and of the web app at outly.si/app, including map tiles. Cloudflare processes the technical data any web server receives when you open a page, such as your IP address and the page or map tile requested.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — error and crash reports from the app, from the web app at outly.si/app and from our server, so we can fix what breaks: the error message and stack trace, time and version; in the app also the device model, iOS version and a random installation identifier that is not linked to your account; in the web app the page address and the browser and device type; from the server the request path without query parameters. No name, email address, account, screenshots or request contents. Sentry sees your IP address when a report arrives but does not store it, nor a location derived from it. Data is stored in the European Union (Germany).

We may also disclose data to public authorities where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some of the providers above are companies based in the United States, so personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or, where it applies to the provider, an adequacy decision. You can request a copy of the safeguards at luka@outly.si.

6. How long we keep it

  • Confirmed waitlist sign-ups: until Outly launches in your city and we have sent you the launch email, and no longer than 24 months from the day you confirmed.
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: deleted no later than 90 days after sign-up if you never confirm.
  • Account data: for as long as you have an account. You can delete it yourself in the app (Account settings), in the web app (My Account → Delete account) or on this website (profile → Delete account).
  • Confirmation and reset codes: valid for a limited time, then invalidated.
  • IP address for rate limiting: only as a hash, deleted about one hour after the first counted request at the latest.
  • Data in your browser (website and web app): as described for each item in section 10; you can remove all of it by clearing site data for outly.si. The ticket list on a club staff device is deleted at log-out, otherwise, like sent scans, the first time the web app is opened after three days.
  • Error and crash reports: 30 days, then deleted automatically by Sentry.
  • Purchase data: as long as tax and accounting rules require. When you delete your account, purchases are detached from you: your email is replaced by an anonymous marker; the amount and date remain for accounting.
  • Creator applications: as long as needed to assess them and, if we work together, for the duration of the business relationship and any statutory retention periods.

7. Children

You must be at least 15 to create an Outly account. That is the age set for information-society services in Slovenia by Article 8 of the Personal Data Protection Act (ZVOP-2). If we learn that a younger child created an account without parental consent, we delete it. Events have their own age limits, set by the organiser and checked at the door.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask what data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted — you can delete your account yourself in the app, in the web app or on this website;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, and object where we rely on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect what we did before.

To exercise any of these, write to luka@outly.si from the address you signed up with, or tell us which address to look for. We answer within one month. Getting off the waitlist takes one sentence — you do not need to give a reason.

9. Complaints

If you think we are handling your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Slovenian supervisory authority: Informacijski pooblaščenec Republike Slovenije, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, Slovenia, gp.ip@ip-rs.si · www.ip-rs.si

10. Cookies, browser storage and third-party content

This website and the web app at outly.si/app do not set advertising or analytics cookies and do not track you across other sites. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or similar tools, so there is no cookie banner. Your browser keeps only what the service you ask for needs:

  • your login session, if you log in, so you stay signed in (one session for the website and the web app); removed when you log out;
  • the invite code, if you came through a friend’s link, so the point goes to the right person when you sign up. It is removed once you join the waitlist;
  • your email address, when you enter it to sign up, reset your password or sign in with a not yet confirmed address in the web app, so you do not have to type it again on the next step (entering the code). It is removed when you sign in or log out in the web app;
  • in the web app also: your language, if you choose one; “My preferences” (genres, distance, age, price), removed when you log out; for club owners, whether the profile shows the club or the personal account;
  • for club staff who scan tickets: the ticket list, scans and a random device marker (section 2, Ticket scanning in the web app);
  • for as long as the tab is open (sessionStorage): temporary data of a purchase in progress, such as an order key that prevents a double purchase when you come back from the payment page. The browser deletes it when you close the tab.

To open faster and work without a connection, the web app stores its own code, typeface and icons in the browser (service worker cache). None of your data, tickets or responses from our server are stored there. If you add the web app to your phone’s home screen, it opens like an app; this does not change what data we process.

You can remove all of this by clearing site data for outly.si in your browser settings. When you log out, we remove the items marked above as removed at log-out.

The typeface and scripts are served from our own domain, not from Google Fonts or a public CDN. The website has no embedded third-party content (videos, maps, social widgets); the Instagram link is a plain link. The web app shows club and event images and videos from Cloudinary’s servers (section 4) and the map from our own domain. Payment takes place on the page of our payment provider Stripe, to which we redirect you.

11. Security

The website, the app and the web app talk to our servers over HTTPS only. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and cannot be recovered. Confirmation and reset codes are time-limited. Our authentication provider Supabase limits sign-in and code verification attempts. In the app, your session token is kept in the phone’s secure system storage; on this website and in the web app it is kept in the browser’s local storage, where code running on outly.si can read it. That is why outly.si loads only our own code from our own domain, with no advertising, analytics or other third-party scripts, and the web app also has a strict Content Security Policy that stops the browser from running code from other domains. Full email addresses on the waitlist are held in a private table the website itself cannot read: the public waitlist on our home page shows only masked addresses such as j***@example.com or a chosen username. Club data is visible only to the club owner and the staff the owner has added.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. Use of the app is governed by the Terms of Use (in Slovenian), which you accept when you create an account.
